# Synthetic school week policy

This fictional policy applies only to the School AI demonstration school.
It is not an official curriculum or advice for a real school.

## Teaching hours and lunch

Teaching takes place Monday to Friday, from 08:00 to 12:00 and 13:00 to 15:00.
Lunch is protected from 12:00 to 13:00. Teachers and rooms are unavailable for
lessons during lunch. The synthetic timetable models one-hour lessons.

## Weekly curriculum and distribution

Each class has 25 lessons per week: Mathematics 5, English 5, Science 4,
History 3, Physical Education 2, Music 2, Computing 2 and Visual Arts 2.
The school prefers five lessons per day and different days for repeated
sessions of the same subject. Daily balance and subject spreading are soft
preferences; resource conflicts and availability remain hard constraints.
A timetable may have an uneven daily workload if restricted availability
requires it. CP-SAT decides feasibility and never relaxes hard constraints.

## Specialist rooms

Science uses the laboratory, Music uses the music studio, and Physical
Education uses the sports hall. Other demo subjects use general classrooms.
Rooms must have enough capacity for the entire student group.
